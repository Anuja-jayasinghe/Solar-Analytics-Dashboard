# SolisCloud investigation: evidence as of 2026-10-06

Read-only investigation at repository commit `ad3a934`. Raw signed API responses
are private under the local user's `.local/share/solis-diagnostics/` and are not
in Git. This report contains aggregate measurements, alarm codes and dates.
Cloud telemetry cannot identify the physical cause of an electrical fault.

## The four Pro warnings

**Confirmed in code:** `src/v3/pro/metrics.js` takes one completed day, keeps
points with `pac_kw > 1`, averages `iPv1` through `iPv8`, and flags each channel
more than 8% below the eight-channel mean. Voltage is averaged for the tooltip
only. `src/v3/pages/ProPage.jsx` uses the last completed day for this panel;
the range selector does not extend this test.

**Confirmed from live API:** PV2, PV4, PV7 and PV8 were flagged on each of seven
completed days, 2026-09-29 through 2026-10-05. Each day had 146–150 inverter
records and 116–133 producing records. The 2026-10-05 mean currents were:

| PV input | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Mean current, A | 5.50 | 0.12 | 5.65 | 0.32 | 5.84 | 6.30 | 0.00 | 0.14 |

At **every producing sample** in those seven days, `uPv1=uPv2`, `uPv3=uPv4`,
`uPv5=uPv6` and `uPv7=uPv8`. The eight API fields are not eight independent
string-voltage readings. The live `machine` field identifies an **S5-GC40K**;
the bundled manufacturer datasheet specifies **four MPPTs and up to eight
string inputs**. The physical input-to-string wiring remains unverified.

**Interpretation:** Pro compares all eight positions as though they are equally
occupied and comparable. Its warning does not establish that four connected
arrays are underperforming. PV5 and PV6 currently carry substantial current at
the same reported voltage. Their pooled median `iPv5/iPv6` ratio was **1.00**
across 507 simultaneous samples above 10 kW AC in the seven-day window.
This is an observation, not a verdict against an unknown string design.

## A change in the reported PV input pattern

**Confirmed:** Sampled days in 2024 and 2025 showed PV1, PV3, PV5 and PV7 as
the high-current positions; PV2, PV4, PV6 and PV8 were low. For example,
2024-10-03 had mean `iPv6=0.22 A`, `iPv7=5.34 A` and median `uPv7=699 V`.
On 2026-04-10, PV6 averaged 0.34 A and PV7 8.01 A. By 2026-04-12, PV6
carried substantial current and PV7 was zero. The four-position Pro warning
persisted, but its fourth flagged position changed from PV6 to PV7.

On **2026-04-11**, the last PV7-current sample was at **13:31:03 Asia/Colombo**.
The next inverter sample was at **17:15:11**, a **224-minute gap**, and showed
PV6 carrying current. At 13:31 the reported `uPv7` had fallen from about
659 V five minutes earlier to **159.6 V**. The separate logger signal endpoint
still recorded **41 samples** during the inverter-telemetry gap. The April
alarm list contains **no April 11 alarm**. The data do not distinguish an
inverter interruption, maintenance, rewiring, configuration change or
telemetry remapping.

**Second change:** PV7 current remained zero after April 11 while `uPv7/uPv8`
were near 220–230 V on sampled days through June 5. On **2026-06-06**, the last reading
above 150 V was **250.7 V at 09:17:50**; the first below 50 V was **8.2 V at
09:33:03**. PV7 current was zero at both readings, and logger signal samples
continued around that interval. Subsequent sampled days showed about 9–15 V.
No June 6 alarm was recorded near the transition. Its physical meaning is
unknown. The owner does not have the as-built string schedule and is unsure
whether work occurred on either date. Installer records, if obtainable, would
help interpret these changes.

## Other health signals

### Grid voltage and alarms

Using observed `pageNo` pagination, `alarmList` returned **105 distinct alarms
from 2026-07-01 through 2026-10-05**: 100 code `1011` (Solis grid
under-voltage), two `1010` (grid over-voltage), and three `1D4C2` logger
internet alerts. Of the under-voltage alerts, 42 began in the 06:00 local
hour, 21 in the 18:00 hour and 17 in the 17:00 hour. Eight lasted over an
hour; alarm duration is not equal to lost daylight generation. All nine
October 1–5 under-voltage alerts had a same-time inverter record at **0 kW**;
several occurred around sunrise or sunset.

Across **873 producing samples** from 2026-09-29 through 2026-10-05, the
reported `uAc1/uAc2/uAc3` phase-voltage medians were **221.8 V / 232.0 V /
246.7 V**. The median simultaneous highest-minus-lowest spread was **25.2 V**;
phase 3 was highest in 815 of 873 samples. At power above 10 kW, the three
phase-current medians were similar: **31.7 A / 32.2 A / 31.5 A**. Pro currently
averages the three AC phase voltages into one value, hiding this spread.
Actual phase-to-neutral voltage at the inverter and grid connection, and the
configured grid standard, must be checked before attributing a cause. The
API cannot distinguish grid conditions, wiring and sensor calibration here.

### F017 AC-side alarms

June records contain **three F017 “L&PE FAIL” alerts**: June 2 at 11:52 and
June 9 at 12:44 and 13:04 local time. Their recorded durations were about
10, 15 and 10 minutes; all are marked recovered. The inverter continued to
report generation during those windows, so a production outage is not
established. [Solis's alarm reference](https://usservice.solisinverters.com/support/solutions/articles/73000560423-solis-inverter-alarm-codes-complete-list-)
describes F017 as low resistance between line and protective earth and calls
for an AC-side wiring/ground check. A qualified installer should review these
events independently of the PV input question.

  corresponding `inverterMonth.energy` values: 150.2, 152.4, 161.8, 148.0
  and 147.2 kWh. This cross-checks two Solis views, not an external meter.
- Recent daily series had a five-minute upload cadence and no gap greater
  than about 5.1 minutes within each sampled day.
- `weatherList`, `epmList` and `ammeterList` each returned zero devices. There
  is no on-site irradiance series in these endpoints to establish expected
  energy or weather-adjusted degradation.

### Historical energy reconciliation: April 2025 archive gap

`inverterAll` returned annual raw energies of **17,708.2 kWh (2024),
45,329.2 kWh (2025), and 36,677.7 kWh (2026 through the query)**. Its
`energyPec=0.001` and `energyStr=MWh` mean that the raw kWh-scale numbers
display in MWh after applying the scale. The three rollups sum to **99,715.1
kWh**. The current `inverterDetail` lifetime counter was about **101,304 kWh**;
these readings have different cutoff times, so their simple difference is
only a signal to reconcile by completed periods.

For 2025, the last 2024-12-31 `inverterDay.eTotal` was **17,760 kWh** and the
last 2025-12-31 value was **64,424 kWh**, a **46,664 kWh** counter increase.
The 2025 `inverterAll` rollup was **45,329.2 kWh**, a **1,334.8 kWh** gap.
Monthly checks localize **1,301.3 kWh** of that gap to April 2025: its
counter rose **4,480 kWh** while `inverterYear` reported **3,178.7 kWh**.

The April `inverterMonth` response has **22 daily records** and no records
for **April 14–21 inclusive**. Targeted `inverterDay` requests for April 14,
17 and 21 each returned no records. The last April 13 counter was **31,643
kWh**; the first April 22 sample reported **33,089 kWh** and `eToday=144.8
kWh`. Subtracting that partial April 22 daily value from the counter step
gives approximately **1,301.2 kWh across the eight missing days**, consistent
with the monthly gap to rounding. This is a *combined counter inference*,
not measured per-day energy; it does not establish the cause of absent cloud
history or independently validate billable energy.

The independent plant endpoints show the **same source-side archive gap**:
`stationMonth` also returned 22 April 2025 dates, omitting April 14–21;
`stationYear` reported **3,178.7 kWh** for April; and `stationAll` reported
**45,329.2 kWh** for 2025. Agreement between plant and inverter rollups
shows the omission is not unique to the inverter rollup endpoint, but both
views come from SolisCloud and do not constitute an external meter check.

The dashboard's data API is designed to count absent daily records as
`missingDays` and label their energy unknown (`shared/data/resources.js`).
The Overview can show the separate inverter lifetime counter
(`src/v3/overview/TotalsRow.jsx`). We have not authenticated to the
dashboard's production data API, so whether its stored April 2025 rows and
missing-day count match the current Solis response remains unverified.

## Current documentation versus live responses

| [Authorization](https://developer.soliscloud.com/guide/authorization.html) specifies `Content-Type: application/json;charset=UTF-8`. | The documented signing/header combination returned HTTP 403 for `inverterList`; the repo's `application/json` combination returned HTTP 200 and API code 0 with the same nonempty body. | Preserve the working signer; the test does not identify which layer produced 403. |
| [`inverterDay`](https://developer.soliscloud.com/guide/data-access-user.html) lists `uPv1–4` and `iPv1–4`. | All eight positions appeared in this S5-GC40K's day records. | The listed fields are incomplete for this inverter. |
| List pagination says `minId`, ascending unique IDs and `total=0`. | `alarmList` advanced with `pageNo=2`; `minId` based on returned nonunique `id` repeated page 1. July–October reported `total=105` and yielded 105 distinct alarms across two pages; `inverterList` reported `total=1`. | Use observed page-number behavior for this account; documented cursor behavior is unverified. |
| `inverterMonth.date` is documented as a string. | `date` was epoch milliseconds; `dateStr` held the calendar date. | Use `dateStr` for day labels. |
| `inverterDay.timeStr` has no timezone explanation. | It was 2 h 30 m ahead of Asia/Colombo time calculated from `dataTimestamp`, consistent with UTC+8. | Use `dataTimestamp` for chronology. |

The developer portal [release notes](https://developer.soliscloud.com/guide/release-notes.html)
include changes through 2026-09-24. These mismatches indicate incomplete or
account-specific documentation, not an abandoned portal.

## Next evidence needed

1. **Physical string map:** terminal used by each string, MPPT grouping,
   panel model, module count, orientation and tilt. This determines whether
   PV2/PV4/PV7/PV8 are unused or faulty inputs.
2. **Service history:** work or configuration changes on April 11 and June 6.
3. **Qualified electrical review:** validate all three AC phase voltages and
   review the under-voltage and F017 alerts. No live wiring changes are part
   of this API investigation.
4. **Dashboard data reconciliation:** compare selected raw timestamps with
   stored normalized telemetry and the actual Pro API response. Raw records
   reproduce the four flagged channel numbers, but the database leg has not
   been independently verified here. Check whether April 14–21, 2025 appear
   as unknown in the stored daily summaries and whether historical billing
   comparisons mark the period incomplete.
5. **Performance reference:** obtain the string map and irradiance or a
   comparable reference before estimating energy loss or module health.
